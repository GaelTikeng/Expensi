# Privacy Policy — xpens-ia

_Draft for legal review. Last updated: 2026-10-04._

## Who we are
xpens-ia is published by Nyota Ltd. Contact: privacy@nyota.ltd.

## What we collect
- **Account**: your email address and name, handled by our sign-in provider
  Clerk. We never see your password.
- **Your expense data**: amounts, dates, descriptions, payees, categories,
  notes, planned expenses and fixed charges you enter.
- **Files you attach or import**: receipt photos, PDFs, and spreadsheets you
  choose to upload.
- **Technical data**: crash reports and performance data when you opt in to
  diagnostics; device timezone and currency preference.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we
do not sell or share your data for advertising.

## How we use it
- To store and show your expenses, recaps and plans.
- To extract line items from spreadsheets and PDFs you import, and to write
  short spending summaries. **These two features send the relevant data to an
  AI processor (TensorX, hosted in the EU with zero data retention).** The
  processor receives only what is needed for the request and does not train
  on your data.
- To send reminders you schedule. Reminders are generated on your device.

## Where it is stored
- Database: Neon (PostgreSQL), region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt).
- Files: Amazon S3, region eu-central-1, private bucket. Files are reachable
  only through short-lived signed links generated for you.

## Your controls
- Edit or delete any expense, plan, file or import at any time.
- **Delete your account** from Settings. This removes your database records
  and every file you uploaded; your sign-in identity is deleted at Clerk.
- Export: contact us to receive a copy of your data.

## Retention
Data is kept while your account exists. Uploads that were never completed are
deleted automatically after 24 hours. Deleted accounts are purged immediately.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 16.

## Changes
We will update this page and the date above when the policy changes.
